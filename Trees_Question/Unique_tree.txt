=========================================================
     REQUIREMENTS TO CONSTRUCT A UNIQUE BINARY TREE
=========================================================

1) You need TWO traversals to construct a unique tree.

2) One of them MUST be:
      - Inorder traversal

3) Valid Combinations that form a Unique Tree:

   ✔ Inorder + Preorder  → Unique Tree
   ✔ Inorder + Postorder → Unique Tree

4) Invalid Combinations (NOT Unique):

   ✘ Preorder + Postorder (Not unique)
   ✘ Inorder alone
   ✘ Preorder alone
   ✘ Postorder alone

---------------------------------------------------------

WHY IS INORDER IMPORTANT?

Inorder gives:
    Left Subtree | Root | Right Subtree

It tells us:
    - What belongs to left
    - What belongs to right

Preorder tells:
    Root | Left | Right

Postorder tells:
    Left | Right | Root

So when we combine:
    - Inorder gives structure
    - Pre/Post gives root order

---------------------------------------------------------

SPECIAL CASE:

If tree is a FULL BINARY TREE
(every node has 0 or 2 children)

Then:
    Preorder + Postorder → Unique Tree

Otherwise:
    Not unique.

---------------------------------------------------------

TIME COMPLEXITY:
    O(n) to build using HashMap for inorder index lookup.

---------------------------------------------------------

INTERVIEW ONE-LINER:

A unique binary tree can be constructed only when
Inorder traversal is combined with either Preorder
or Postorder traversal.
